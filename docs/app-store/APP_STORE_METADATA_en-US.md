## Description

PickerRoy turns videos into a focused gallery of photo-worthy moments without uploading your footage.

Import a video, choose an output aspect ratio, shortlist useful photographs, and save them locally. Spend your time shooting and creating instead of dragging a timeline frame by frame.

### A shortlist that learns your taste

PickerRoy learns from your decisions on your own device, gradually adapting video-frame recommendations to your shooting habits and visual taste. Meaningful preference feedback builds a local profile for later recommendations. Whether your work is outdoors, portraits, or landscapes, use your own footage to express your own eye.

Personalization depends on useful comparisons, not simply importing more videos. It does not guarantee that every later recommendation is more accurate. You remain the final editor.

### Enhance, with a natural finish

Keep the original look with Direct export or choose Enhance. Depending on each image, it gently adjusts flat contrast, usable shadow tones, and muted colors. Only suitable frames receive a small amount of sharpening; it never applies strong sharpening indiscriminately.

Pixel dimensions stay the same, and no missing detail is generated. Enhance is about a considered presentation, not higher resolution. An already suitable original may change very little. Everything is processed on your device; enhancement takes additional time compared with Direct export.

### From shortlist to saved photo

PickerRoy evaluates people, scenery, composition, sharpness, exposure, and color locally to help with the first pass. Pause, resume, or safely cancel an analysis, and confirm your final choices before export.

Highlights:

- Person, landscape, and detail frame selection
- Original, 1:1, 2:3, 3:4, 16:9, and 9:16 output ratios
- Pause, resume, and cancel during analysis
- Direct full-quality frame export
- Enhance for adaptive tone and color with a natural presentation
- Local preference learning for recommendations that gradually reflect your eye
- No account, ads, tracking, or video uploads
- Native Mac experience first, with iPhone to follow (adjust to the platform being submitted)

Results depend on the source video, compression, noise, and crop. Enhance is not super-resolution and cannot repair severe defocus. Not every picture is guaranteed to look better.
