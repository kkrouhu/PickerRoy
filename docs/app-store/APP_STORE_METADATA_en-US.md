## Description

PickerRoy turns videos into a focused gallery of photo-worthy moments without uploading your footage.

Import a video, choose an output aspect ratio, and let PickerRoy evaluate people, scenery, composition, sharpness, exposure, and color on your device. Pause, resume, or safely cancel an analysis whenever you need to.

Every Like and Show Less decision helps build a private preference profile. The more you use PickerRoy, the closer its recommendations move toward your own visual taste. Your preference profile stays on the device.

Highlights:

- Person, landscape, and detail frame selection
- Original, 1:1, 2:3, 3:4, 16:9, and 9:16 output ratios
- Pause, resume, and cancel during analysis
- Direct full-quality frame export
- Optional enhanced export for gentle resizing, color, and sharpness improvements
- On-device personalization
- No account, ads, tracking, or video uploads
- Native iPhone, iPad, and Mac experience

Enhanced export cannot recreate real detail that is absent from the source video. Results still depend on source resolution, compression, and crop.

PickerRoy V1.0 is free to download and all current core features are available. This release has no subscription, In-App Purchase, paywall, purchase button, or restore-purchase control.
