# PickerRoy Privacy Policy

Updated September 15, 2026 (release draft; verify before public launch).

PickerRoy is designed to keep your videos and choices on your device.

## Data we do not collect

PickerRoy does not collect, upload, sell, or share your videos, exported images, filenames, file paths, information about people, usage activity, device identifiers, location, contacts, or visual preferences. PickerRoy contains no advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK, or cross-app tracking technology.

## On-device processing

Videos you explicitly select are processed on the device. The native Apple version uses system media and vision frameworks; the standalone desktop test version uses bundled local video engines. Candidate previews, analysis records, and preferences are stored in local app data; exported images are written to the location you choose. On iPhone, PickerRoy requests add-only Photos access only when you save selected images; it does not read your full photo library. None of this data is sent to PickerRoy or any third party.

After first-import acknowledgment of the media-use notice, only the notice version and acknowledgment time are stored locally, with no account association or transmission to us. Acknowledgment does not mean we verified media ownership.

## Network access

Core features do not require an internet connection. The app does not connect to a server to upload videos or preference data. App downloads, operating-system updates, and support webpages are provided by Apple or your browser.

## Children's privacy

PickerRoy does not create user accounts and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Deleting local data

You can delete exported images yourself. The native Apple version offers a local preference-reset control; the desktop test version's manual explains data locations, backup, and deletion. Removing the Mac app alone generally does not remove caches, logs, or preferences under Application Support; these app-specific data must be removed separately. On iPhone, deleting the app removes its container, while offloading retains data. Images saved to Photos or Files must be deleted separately. You remain in control of the source videos. Copies created by system backups or cloud photo/file synchronization are governed by your system settings and service providers.

## Media rights

Use only media you are entitled to process and check the permissions needed to extract and publish images. Users are responsible under applicable law for unlawful or infringing use. See the [media and use notice](../MATERIAL_USE_NOTICE_zh-EN.md); it does not exclude liability that cannot legally be excluded.

## Changes and contact

If future features change these data practices, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before those features are released.
